Getting Started & Provider Matching

During your brief initial onboarding intake, our algorithm assesses your primary clinical goals (e.g. panic regulation, trauma recovery, insomnia, relationship distress), your preferred therapeutic orientation (CBT, MBSR, ACT, EMDR), and state licensure requirements. You can review full psychologist credentials, video introductions, and verified patient reviews before selecting a provider.

Your first consultation is a warm, collaborative diagnostic exploration. Your clinician will explore your symptoms, life stressors, somatic manifestations, and personal values. Together, you will co-design a personalized 8- to 12-week Care Protocol with clear milestone checkpoints.

Every practitioner on HumanCare undergoes rigorous multi-layer credentialing, including primary source state board verification, NPDB background checks, CAQH attestation, and PSYPACT interstate telehealth authorization. We do not permit uncredentialed wellness coaches to deliver clinical psychotherapy.

Telehealth & Video Consultations

No. All telehealth video consultations run directly inside your modern web browser via WebRTC with end-to-end AES-256 encryption. Simply click "Join Telehealth Room" from your Patient Sanctuary dashboard 5 minutes prior to appointment time.

We understand unexpected life events happen. You may cancel or reschedule any appointment without penalty up to 24 hours prior to scheduled start time via your Appointments dashboard. Late cancellations within 24 hours may incur a clinician reservation fee.

Billing, Insurance & HSA/FSA

Yes! Licensed psychotherapy and psychiatric evaluations are 100% qualified medical expenses under IRS Code 213(d). You may enter your HSA/FSA card directly during appointment booking.

Following each completed session, an automated itemized Superbill with standard CPT procedure codes (90834, 90837) and ICD-10 diagnostic codes is automatically generated in your Patient Portal under "Billing & Statements" for instant 1-click download.
