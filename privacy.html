Clinical Privacy & HIPAA Safeguards

Your therapeutic disclosures, somatic telemetry, and private reflections are protected by stringent healthcare privacy standards and cryptographic isolation.

1. HIPAA & HITECH Compliance Foundation

HumanCare operates in full compliance with the Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act of 1996 (HIPAA) and the Health Information Technology for Economic and Clinical Health (HITECH) Act. We maintain strict Administrative, Physical, and Technical safeguards to ensure the confidentiality, integrity, and availability of all electronic Protected Health Information (ePHI).

Business Associate Agreements (BAA):

Every technical vendor, infrastructure hosting provider, and certified telehealth transport protocol maintains signed BAAs adhering to 45 CFR § 164.502(e) and § 164.504(e).

2. Phase 1 In-Memory Ephemeral Storage Guarantee

In accordance with our strict privacy-first architecture for Phase 1, sensitive mental health notes, emotional journal reflections, subjective distress units (SUDS), and AI conversational exchanges are stored strictly in volatile browser RAM memory (`window.mindcareState`).

  • No Unencrypted LocalStorage: Journal narratives and sensitive clinical check-ins are NEVER written to persistent browser cookies or localStorage.
  • No External Telemetry Tracking: Phase 1 features no third-party tracking scripts, advertising beacons, or pixel trackers (e.g. Meta Pixel, TikTok, or Google Ads tags are strictly excluded).
  • Session Volatility: When you close your browser tab or sign out, sensitive in-memory reflections are immediately wiped from memory.

3. Telehealth Encryption & PSYPACT Standards

All peer-to-peer telehealth sessions are conducted through encrypted WebRTC protocols utilizing Datagram Transport Layer Security (DTLS) and Secure Real-time Transport Protocol (SRTP) with AES-256 bit symmetric key encryption.

All clinical psychologists providing telehealth across state lines are credentialed through PSYPACT (Psychology Interjurisdictional Compact) with active Authority to Practice Interjurisdictional Telepsychology (APIT) numbers, adhering to the ethical standards of the American Psychological Association (APA).

4. GDPR & Patient Data Sovereignty

Regardless of your geographic location, HumanCare extends comprehensive patient data sovereignty rights:

Right of Access & Portability

Patients may request a complete machine-readable JSON export of their clinical history and Superbills at any time.

Right to Erasure ("Forgotten")

Patients may request permanent account purge subject to mandatory clinical record retention laws (typically 7 years).

5. Mandatory Safety & Duty-to-Protect Exceptions

While clinician-patient privilege is sacred, licensed mental health professionals are legally and ethically bound by federal and state Mandated Reporting statutes in the following isolated circumstances:

  • Imminent risk of severe physical self-harm or suicide where emergency stabilization is urgently required.
  • Reasonable suspicion or knowledge of child abuse, neglect, or elder exploitation.
  • Clear and imminent threat of physical violence directed toward a designated individual (Tarasoff duty to warn).
