How Vagus Pacing Works

When your exhale is intentionally elongated (e.g. 7-8 seconds), your thoracic pressure rises, prompting baroreceptors to signal the vagus nerve. This directly slows sinoatrial heart rate firing and signals safety to the amygdala.

Optimal Body Posture
  • Keep spine upright but without rigid straining.
  • Rest tongue against the roof of mouth behind front teeth.
  • Inhale purely through the nose; exhale through lightly pursed lips with a gentle 'whoosh' sound.
